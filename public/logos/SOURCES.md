## BetterCorella logo

The BetterCorella logo and icons (`public/favicon.*`, `public/apple-touch-icon.png`, `public/pwa-*.png`, `public/og.png`, `src/assets/logo*.png`) are project artwork. It follows the BetterInabanga / Better San Fernando style.

- **Map:** the Corella municipal boundary from the PSA/NAMRIA administrative boundaries (2023-11-06, ADM3 `PH0701215`), via [faeldon/philippines-psgc-shapefiles](https://github.com/faeldon/philippines-psgc-shapefiles), filled in Philippine blue `#0038A8`.
- **Sun:** the sun of the Philippine flag, gold `#FCD116`.
- **Wordmark:** [Plus Jakarta Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans) ExtraBold (SIL Open Font License 1.1), converted to outlines.

This is not the official seal of the Municipality of Corella and must not be presented as one.
